## Medium 用 (English)

**Title**: Growing to 54 Supabase Edge Functions: Notification Center, Onboarding Flow & AI Secretary in One Day

**Tags**: Flutter, Supabase, TypeScript, AI, BuildInPublic

**Lead**:
I'm building "Jibun Inc." (自分株式会社), an AI-integrated life management app with Flutter Web + Supabase, aiming to consolidate features from 21 competing SaaS products. Today I hit a milestone: 54 Edge Functions deployed.

**Key implementations**:
- **notification-center**: In-app notification management with 7 categories and RLS
- **onboarding-flow**: 6-step auto-onboarding with DB-based completion detection
- **ai-secretary**: Enhanced with 12-department business knowledge + stats-based replies
- **4 bug fixes** including the Supabase `.update().order().limit()` gotcha

**Gotcha of the day**:
Supabase JS client's `.update()` combined with `.order()` and `.limit()` throws a runtime error — PostgreSQL doesn't support `UPDATE ... ORDER BY ... LIMIT`. Switch to INSERT for logging-style updates.
